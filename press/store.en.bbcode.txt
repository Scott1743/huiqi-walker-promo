[h1]灰契行者[/h1]

[h2]One new weapon. A whole new game plan.[/h2]

A single-player, loot-driven card RPG. Build a twenty-card deck, find the gear that makes it yours, and carry your growing collection into the next battle.

[h2]Keep the cards. Choose the moment.[/h2]

Regular cards stay in your hand between turns. Manage energy, read enemy intent, and decide which minions to trade across five friendly slots. Play spells in the right order, create temporary Echoes, and turn Combo into an opening. Unused Echoes disappear at the end of the turn.

[h2]Find the equipment that changes your plan.[/h2]

Your weapon, armor, trinket, and seal work together. A core weapon can spread burn damage to adjacent minions, spend armor on a hero attack, or create an Echo of an eligible spell. Affixes support the way you want to play. Compare your drops and build around the combinations you keep.

There are 28 equipment types: 16 equipment bases and 12 core items. These are different loadouts, not three weapons equipped at once.

[h2]Bring something back.[/h2]

Cards and gear you have collected stay yours, even if an expedition ends in defeat or retreat. Clear a chapter for gold and a choice of three rewards. Buy five-card packs with earned gold, break the seal, and reveal the results one card at a time. Copies above the collection limit become gold.

The current collection contains 96 cards. Find new combinations, edit your twenty-card deck, and prepare for another expedition.

[h2]Three chapters. Different ways to break through.[/h2]

In the Ember Mines, destroy the kilns powering the boss. In the Mirror Workshop, break mirrors that copy your minions. In the Sealed Archives, destroy the index page to cancel the announced main action; enemy minions can still attack. Progress through Story, Elite, and Master to pursue different chapter equipment pools.

[h2]Take your build into Endless.[/h2]

Clear all three chapters on Story to unlock Endless. Your deck and equipment lock when the run begins. Face a boss every fifth floor, push your personal best, and bank your score when the run ends.

Redeem settled points for ten animated card backs, alongside the classic default. These cosmetics do not change stats or draw order, and spending points does not reduce your personal best.
