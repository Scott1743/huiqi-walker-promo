# 灰契行者

### From the ashes, reforge your strategy

The same hand can offer a different answer when a new item joins your loadout.

In 灰契行者, the spoils of an expedition become part of your next build. Find a piece of gear worth keeping and pair it with familiar cards. A battle that once held you back may reveal a new opening.

### Give your equipment a place in your tactics

Use a wildfire staff to consume stored burn and deal damage to adjacent minions. Wield a siege hammer and weigh the armor you need to survive against the armor you can spend on a hero attack. With a mirror baton, an eligible spell leaves an echo: reserve the energy and extend your turn with a second cast.

Gear changes targets, positioning, card order, and the way you spend your resources. Shape a deck around a core item, or find the equipment that brings your favorite cards together.

### Hold your hand. Choose your moment.

Regular cards stay in your hand between turns. Stabilize the board, keep a key spell, and wait for an opening worth your resources.

Across five minion slots, taunt protects your setup and careful trades clear a path. Bring fire, armor, and echoes together in a strategy of your own. Temporary echoes disappear at the end of the turn. You must balance what can wait against what needs to happen now.

### The expedition ends. Your progress remains.

Loot already in your inventory stays yours, even if you fall in battle or retreat.

Return to the workshop, compare new equipment, and adjust your cards. After clearing a chapter, choose from three rewards offering equipment or replacement gold. Spend your gold on card packs and open new cards to expand your collection and your next build.

Every return leaves something to carry into the next battle.

Charge the wax seal, break it open, and reveal five cards one by one. Each rarity has its own light and sound; new cards join your collection, while copies beyond the collection limit turn into gold.

### Three chapters. Three defenses to unravel.

Furnaces power the mine’s boss. Mirrors copy your latest minion. An index page announces the archive’s next main action.

Destroy the furnaces, break the mirrors, and interrupt the index. Test your build against a different mechanic in each chapter. Each chapter has separate equipment pools for Story, Elite, and Master. Unlock higher difficulties and pursue their exclusive gear.

Blue-black woodcut battlefields and pale paper cards set the stage for each decision. **From the ashes, reforge your strategy.**

### Game features

- 20-card decks and five friendly minion slots, with regular cards retained between turns.
- 48 collectible cards and 6 token types, with three schools you can mix.
- Four gear slots, 16 equipment bases, 16 affixes, and 12 core items.
- Three expedition chapters and three bosses with distinct mechanics; Story, Elite, and Master unlock through progression.
- Ten editable build recipes, permanent loot, a choice of three chapter rewards, and card packs purchased with earned gold.
