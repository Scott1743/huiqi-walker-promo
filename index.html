BUILD AROUND THE LOOT YOU FIND

The same cards. A new way to play.

A weapon can change your targets, your timing, and the cards you keep. Shape a strategy that fits the gear you bring.

契

Hold your hand. Choose your moment.

Regular cards stay in your hand between turns. Manage energy, read enemy intent, and plan trades across five minion slots. Temporary Echoes disappear at turn’s end.

20-card decks · 96 collectible cards
铸

Change your gear. Change the plan.

Combine weapon, armor, trinket, and seal. Spread burn damage, spend armor on a hero attack, or create an Echo of an eligible spell.

Four slots · 28 equipment types · 12 core items
归

The expedition ends. Your loot stays.

Keep collected gear and cards. Choose a chapter reward, spend earned gold on five-card packs, and adjust your next deck. Only copies above the collection limit turn into gold.

Permanent collection · Pack opening · New builds
THREE CHAPTERS. DISTINCT BOSS MECHANICS.

Break their defenses. Find your opening.

Take on Story, Elite, and Master as you progress. Each chapter rewards preparation and a different tactical answer.

CHAPTER 1

Ember Mines

Destroy the furnaces powering the boss and make room for your counterattack.

CHAPTER 2

Mirror Workshop

Break mirrors that copy your minions. Reconsider what to play, and when.

CHAPTER 3

Sealed Archives

Destroy the index page to cancel the announced main action. Enemy minions can still attack.

GO FURTHER. MAKE IT YOURS.

Endless challenges. Animated rewards.

Clear all three chapters on Story to unlock Endless. Your deck and loadout lock when the run begins, with a boss every fifth floor.

Push your personal best

Play through successive battles and bank your score when the run ends. Return to camp to prepare your next attempt.

Earn a new look

Redeem settled points for 10 animated card backs, alongside the classic default. They change the look of your cards, never their stats or draw order.
