## Current development content

| System | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Cards | 96 collectible cards; 20-card decks; five friendly minion slots |
| Equipment | Weapon, armor, trinket, seal; 28 equipment types: 16 bases and 12 core items |
| Campaign | Three chapters with distinct boss mechanics and Story, Elite, and Master progression |
| Collection | Permanent cards and gear; earned-gold five-card packs; excess copies convert to gold |
| Endless | Unlock by clearing each of the three chapters on Story; deck and loadout lock at entry; a boss every fifth floor |
| Card backs | 10 animated designs plus the classic default; redeem settled Endless points; cosmetic only |
